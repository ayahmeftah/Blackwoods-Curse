### Objectives

- Escape the mansion.
- Solve puzzles to unlock new areas.
- Collect and use key items.
- Find hidden collectibles to boost your score.
- Survive timed challenges.
- Uncover one of several possible endings.

### How It Works

- Explore the environment and investigate everything.
- Pick up, examine, and combine objects.
- Watch for clues in light, sound, and item placement.
- Some sections are timed—stay alert and move fast.
- Collectibles are hidden throughout the game and affect your score.

### Hints

- Grimm the Cat appears at certain moments to offer helpful guidance.
- On-screen text will point you in the right direction when you're exploring important areas.
- There are no direct tutorials—discovery is part of the experience.

### Tips & Warnings

- Use your flashlight to reveal hidden clues.
- Listen closely—sound often reveals danger or solutions.
- Contains flashing lights and sudden horror sounds.
- Headphones are recommended for full immersion.
